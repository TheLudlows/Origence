## Disclose metric differences

PRD business success rate divides independently passed items by independently decided items. Verification coverage divides independently decided items by items due for verification. Report pending count separately. For three due items with one pass, one fail, and one pending, rates are 1/2 and 2/3. Use null for zero denominators. Offline scoring uses all preregistered cases for `task_success_rate` and the frozen required check count for `verification_coverage`. Checks missing independent verification remain in the denominator. For formal sign-off, report the PRD denominators separately with the basis for independent judgments.
